# Zombies With Horns (Forge 1.20.1)

Tactical undead commanders! Certain zombies spawn equipped with a vanilla Goat Horn, sounding battle calls that rally and enrage nearby monsters.

---

## 🌟 Features

* **Horn Sounding AI**: Upon acquiring a player target, horn-bearing zombies sound their horn, playing authentic vanilla horn audio.
* **Undead Reinforcements**: All nearby zombies, skeletons, and hostile mobs within an audio radius are alerted, locking onto the detected player.
* **Cooldown Management**: Balanced horn intervals prevent audio spam while maintaining high combat tension.
